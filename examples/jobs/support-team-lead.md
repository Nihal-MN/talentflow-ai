# Support Team Lead

**Company:** Lighthouse Cargo · **Location:** Dubai, UAE · **Type:** Full-time

## About the role

We are looking for a Support Team Lead to run our 12-person customer support
team for freight-forwarding customers. You will own quality, tooling and
coaching while staying close to the frontline.

## What you will do

- Lead, coach and schedule a team of 12 support specialists across two shifts.
- Own support KPIs (first response, resolution time, CSAT) and report weekly to leadership.
- Improve processes and macros in Zendesk; collaborate with engineering on product feedback.
- Handle escalated customer issues end to end and drive root-cause fixes.

## Requirements

- 3+ years in customer support for logistics, SaaS or marketplace products.
- 1+ year of team lead, mentoring or supervisory experience.
- Strong Excel skills for reporting; experience with a ticketing tool (Zendesk, Freshdesk or similar).
- Excellent written communication in English.

## Nice to have

- Experience with SQL or BI tools (Power BI, Looker Studio) for self-serve reporting.
- Familiarity with freight-forwarding operations.
